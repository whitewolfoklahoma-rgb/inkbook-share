Help

What InkBook does

InkBook is a tattoo studio's day book: Day Book runs today's sessions, Pieces tracks each tattoo from consult to healed, Clients holds contact and history, Deposits logs money held against appointments, the Consent Log records which form version each client signed and when, and Artists & Rent splits station rent and commissions. The Dashboard pulls the day together.

Where your data lives

Records live only in this browser on this device — no account, no cloud, nothing uploaded. Back up from Settings & Backup → Export backup (JSON); if this device is lost, the book is gone without a backup. Import backup restores that file on any device. Export weekly.

Honest limits

InkBook records consent forms, deposits, and sessions — it does not collect signatures, process payments, or send reminders. Signed paper/digital forms stay wherever you file them today; this is the log that proves they exist. It is a record-keeping aid, not legal or health-department advice: your consent forms and studio practices still have to satisfy your local rules.
